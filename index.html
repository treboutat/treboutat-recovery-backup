Treboutat Recovery Backup

A private recovery utility for its owner. It creates encrypted, versioned copies of selected local files and application state in the owner’s Google Drive, and checks that saved data can be restored.

The utility uses restic for encryption and versioning and rclone for Google Drive access. It requests only Google’s drive.file permission for files created or explicitly used with this application.

Backup contents are encrypted before upload. This page does not provide access to the backup or accept sign-ups.

Read the privacy policy and terms of use. Support contact is the owner’s support address shown on the Google consent screen.
